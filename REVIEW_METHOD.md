# Review method and provenance

[Home](README.md)

## The lens

We prioritize the move that changes a difficult question into one with a better mathematical handle. “Tractable” may mean a tractable intermediate lemma or representation, not a practical algorithm for the original problem. An undecidability result and a hardness result must not be described as algorithms that solve the underlying task.

For each paper, ask:

1. What is the exact claim, including quantifiers and computational model?
2. What obstacle blocks the obvious approach?
3. What intermediate object or change of viewpoint is introduced?
4. What property survives the transition, and which new tool becomes available?
5. Which lemma is the load-bearing hinge?
6. What was actually checked, and what remains open in this review?

## Three separate kinds of status

| Dimension | Current values | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reading | Reviewed / queued | A review exists, or the paper remains on the title-based queue. |
| Provenance | Retained conversation review / reconstructed source overview | Whether a prior detailed review was retained or a new summary was built from the current introduction and overview. |
| Proof validation | Not independently verified | No complete proof audit or external certification is claimed. |

“Reviewed” does not mean “proved correct.” A reconstruction is not a quotation of an earlier answer. Source hashes identify the PDF bytes checked when creating this repository; they do not prove that every earlier review used the same revision.

## Editorial judgments

Bridge cards are interpretations, not claims about a model’s internal thought process. Cross-field connections alone do not establish novelty: each area may already have a long tradition of interacting with the others. Record a novelty claim only after comparison with the relevant literature.

No practicality scores are maintained. Where computational assumptions affect the theorem's meaning—exact real arithmetic, nonuniform coefficients, an enormous polynomial exponent—they belong in the review.

## Preservation gaps

- The original wording of the first seven identifiable reviews was unavailable. Current overviews were used to reconstruct explicitly labeled entries.
- The latest three reviews were retained in the conversation record and edited into repository form.
- The earlier unidentified “number 2” is unresolved.
- Original review dates and original source revisions are not known. The compilation date is recorded instead.

## Updating a claim

When a source changes, retain the old pinned identity in history and explain the difference in the changelog. Distinguish an author's revision, a discovered proof gap, a corrected reading, and a change in our interpretation. Do not overwrite one as if it were another.
