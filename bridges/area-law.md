# Measure information and energy through one construction

**Paper:** A two-dimensional area law from a global spectral gap

[Full review](../reviews/area-law.md) · [All bridges](README.md)

**Areas connected:** quantum many-body theory · representation theory · information theory

**Obstacle.** A global spectral gap does not directly bound entanglement across arbitrary cuts.

**Bridge.** Quasi-local positive constraints and symmetric replicas permit a common interpolation that compares information gain with excitation energy. Buffers and moving partitions translate this to boundary entropy.

**What becomes manageable.** A global spectral statement can constrain local information accumulation.

**Hinge to check.** Uniform constants, preservation of the gap, and the step from intermediate information bounds to area scaling.

**Reusable question.** Can two quantities that seem incomparable be represented as costs of the same process?

**Status:** Interpretation of the manuscript strategy; not proof verification or evidence of the model’s internal reasoning. See the linked review for provenance and scope.
