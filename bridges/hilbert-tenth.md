# Exclude nonstandard escape routes by growth

**Paper:** Hilbert’s tenth problem over the rational numbers

[Full review](../reviews/hilbert-tenth.md) · [All bridges](README.md)

**Areas connected:** logic · elliptic curves · local arithmetic · height geometry

**Obstacle.** Rational solutions to finite tests may mimic integer behavior without yielding ordinary integers.

**Bridge.** Compactness creates a candidate model; elliptic points encode indices; local congruences and a global height estimate force a growth restriction incompatible with quadratic elliptic heights.

**What becomes manageable.** A sequence of oracle tests can detect integer solvability without first defining all integers existentially in the rationals.

**Hinge to check.** The uniform height bound and its companion-theorem dependencies.

**Reusable question.** Can rigidity rule out every false model more easily than a direct definition characterizes every true object?

**Status:** Interpretation of the manuscript strategy; not proof verification or evidence of the model’s internal reasoning. See the linked review for provenance and scope.
