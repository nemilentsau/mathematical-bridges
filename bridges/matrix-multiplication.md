# Separate shared blocks, then constrain their growth

**Paper:** An Upper Bound of 9/4 for the Matrix Multiplication Exponent

[Full review](../reviews/matrix-multiplication.md) · [All bridges](README.md)

**Areas connected:** tensor algebra · finite Fourier analysis · numerical invariants

**Obstacle.** Shared tensor variables prevent useful blocks from being counted as independent summands.

**Bridge.** Fourier labels and a squared-mismatch degeneration isolate matches. Tensor characters then turn the resulting constructions into growth inequalities for a numerical profile.

**What becomes manageable.** An asymptotic exponent is constrained by a lower-growth versus upper-growth comparison.

**Hinge to check.** Exact block isolation and complete accounting for every auxiliary factor.

**Reusable question.** Can labeling convert overlap into independence, after which a scalar invariant exposes the asymptotic consequence?

**Status:** Interpretation of the manuscript strategy; not proof verification or evidence of the model’s internal reasoning. See the linked review for provenance and scope.
