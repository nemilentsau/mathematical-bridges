# Regularize while preserving the forbidden event

**Paper:** The Euclidean plane is not five-colorable

[Full review](../reviews/plane-coloring.md) · [All bridges](README.md)

**Areas connected:** graph coloring · ergodic harmonic analysis · topology

**Obstacle.** An arbitrary coloring can be too pathological for measure theory or topology.

**Bridge.** Invariant averaging and spectral rigidity produce a weak measurable coloring that retains the zero unit-distance correlation. Local palettes and connected interfaces then expose a geometric contradiction.

**What becomes manageable.** An unrestricted discrete existence question becomes accessible to analytic and topological tools.

**Hinge to check.** Preservation of the forbidden configuration through projection and almost-everywhere exceptions.

**Reusable question.** Can we discard pathological detail while preserving precisely the obstruction the proof needs?

**Status:** Interpretation of the manuscript strategy; not proof verification or evidence of the model’s internal reasoning. See the linked review for provenance and scope.
