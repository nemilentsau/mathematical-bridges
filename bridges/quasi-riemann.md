# Force two transforms to agree on one signal

**Paper:** The Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis: A Zero-Free Half-Plane Re s > 7/8

[Full review](../reviews/quasi-riemann.md) · [All bridges](README.md)

**Areas connected:** automorphic forms · analytic number theory · harmonic analysis

**Obstacle.** Excluding every zero requires more than an average zero-density estimate.

**Bridge.** Reflection bounds a completed theta sum directly; Poisson summation identifies in that same sum a signal involving the reciprocal of the target L-function. Uniform power savings enable analytic continuation beyond the supposed rightmost zeros.

**What becomes manageable.** A zero-exclusion problem becomes compatible estimates for one deliberately constructed auxiliary sum.

**Hinge to check.** Principal residues, exceptional rows, and positive margins uniform across the entire family.

**Reusable question.** Can an object be designed so two exact representations turn a hidden obstruction into a quantitative contradiction?

**Status:** Interpretation of the manuscript strategy; not proof verification or evidence of the model’s internal reasoning. See the linked review for provenance and scope.
