# No shortcut would imply an impossible price

**Paper:** Finite tensor savings and exact Fourier circuits

[Full review](../reviews/tensor-fourier.md) · [All bridges](README.md)

**Areas connected:** linear circuits · convex duality · algebraic geometry · spectral constructions

**Obstacle.** No explicit finite tensor shortcut is available to amplify.

**Bridge.** Assume every shortcut fails. Separation and compactness manufacture a universal cost invariant. A reflection receives incompatible prices from its structured and sparse realizations.

**What becomes manageable.** An existential search becomes an obstruction argument; the resulting finite saving is amplified and transferred to selected Fourier lengths.

**Hinge to check.** The passage from failed finite programs to the invariant, especially feedback and specialization without assumed continuity.

**Reusable question.** Does universal failure force a dual object rigid enough to contradict using two representations of one construction?

**Status:** Interpretation of the manuscript strategy; not proof verification or evidence of the model’s internal reasoning. See the linked review for provenance and scope.
