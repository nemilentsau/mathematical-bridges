# Preserve a nonlinear signal while linear probes stay sensitive

**Paper:** The Unique Games Theorem

[Full review](../reviews/unique-games.md) · [All bridges](README.md)

**Areas connected:** hardness of approximation · finite-field algebra · Fourier analysis

**Obstacle.** Improving completeness by reducing noise can also destroy the soundness test's ability to detect structure.

**Bridge.** An equivariant nonlinear gadget is highly stable under a tailored perturbation, while sufficiently high-rank linear observations remain detectably unstable. Fourier analysis transfers this separation to an existing inverse theorem.

**What becomes manageable.** Completeness and soundness can be controlled by different responses to the same noise.

**Hinge to check.** Rank thresholds and independence survive the full parameter order and decoder conditioning.

**Reusable question.** Can the intended signal and the adversarial probes be made to respond fundamentally differently to one perturbation?

**Status:** Interpretation of the manuscript strategy; not proof verification or evidence of the model’s internal reasoning. See the linked review for provenance and scope.
